VIApass — Palais des Festivals (Cannes)

Network Operations High-Density Wi-Fi & Segmentation Mapping & Playbooks

Summary

This section presents my B2 internship completed at VIApass, responsible for the connectivity of the Palais des Festivals (Cannes). I describe the context, environment, contributions (unified mapping, "event" view, playbooks), and experience feedback focused on readability and action.

Confidentiality: sensitive information (public IPs, SSIDs, identifiers) are anonymized or reformulated.

Educational Objectives

  • Applying network knowledge: VLANs, QoS, high-density Wi-Fi, actionable monitoring.
  • Structuring operational documentation ("public" diagrams, checklists, playbooks).
  • Improving incident management: better qualified tickets, clear decision path.
  • Strengthening Field ↔ NOC communication (symptoms vs. metrics, prioritization).
